1. Introduction and Rationale

For many years, the teachers of Angela Elementary School have faced a recurring challenge: the overwhelming amount of paperwork and the lack of a centralized system for data management. Assessment results, narrative reports, and accomplishment reports are usually saved in scattered folders, flash drives, or personal email accounts of individual teachers. This results in a phenomenon where "scattered files" are difficult to find, easily lost, or accidentally overwritten.

Furthermore, the manual consolidation of these reports by program coordinators and the school head consumes valuable time that could have been spent on actual teaching and student intervention. Miscalculations in reading profiles and delays in report approvals also affect the timely implementation of programs like ARAL.

Recognizing these pain points, the AES Unified Reporting System was conceptualized. It was designed to be a centralized, web-based, and paperless platform where teachers can submit their data seamlessly, coordinators can review them efficiently, and the school head can generate consolidated reports in just a few clicks. By bringing all reporting processes into one unified system, we eliminate the clutter, reduce human error, and ensure that every child's assessment data is secure, accessible, and ready for intervention.

2. Problems Solved by the System

  1. Scattered and Misplaced Files: No more searching for lost files. All reports are stored in one secure Google Sheet database.
  2. Paper Overload (Paperless): No need to stack hundreds of physical paper reports. Everything is saved digitally in the cloud.
  3. Miscalculations: Automatic and accurate computation of Reading Profiles (Frustration, Instructional, Independent) and Math Levels.
  4. Delayed Approvals: Teachers can immediately see in "My Submissions" if their report is approved, returned for correction, or still pending.
  5. Disorganized Data: The "Consolidated Report" automatically combines data from all grade levels without manual typing.

3. Educational and Legal Basis

The algorithms, criteria, and reading/math profiles integrated into this system are not arbitrary. They are strictly aligned with the official guidelines and policies of the Department of Education (DepEd) to ensure accuracy and standard compliance.

A. PHIL-IRI and CRLA

The computation of Frustration, Instructional, and Independent reading profiles is based on DepEd Memorandum No. 173, s. 2019 (Hamon: Bawat Bata Bumabasa) and the official guidelines of the Philippine Informal Reading Inventory (PHIL-IRI) and Comprehensive Rapid Literacy Assessment (CRLA). The system automatically uses DepEd's standard scoring to determine the learner's level, eliminating the risk of manual miscalculations.

B. RMA (Rapid Mathematics Assessment)

For Mathematics, the determination of Highly Proficient, Proficient, Developing, Low Proficient, and Non-Proficient levels is based on the DepEd Rapid Mathematics Assessment (RMA) framework. It utilizes the percentage of correct answers against the total number of items to generate accurate descriptive ratings.

C. ARAL Program

The Academic Recovery and Accessible Learning (ARAL) Program is in accordance with DepEd Order No. 013, s. 2023. The system automatically tags learners as "ARAL Candidates" (in Reading or Math) if they fall under the "Frustration" or "Low Proficient/Non-Proficient" levels. This ensures immediate identification and provision of necessary interventions for learners at risk or struggling in reading and numeracy.

4. Definition of Terms

To ensure a common understanding among all users, the following terms are defined as they are used within the system:

PHIL-IRI (Philippine Informal Reading Inventory)

An assessment tool used to determine the reading proficiency of students in terms of word recognition and comprehension.

CRLA (Comprehensive Rapid Literacy Assessment)

A rapid assessment tool used for early literacy (Grades 1-3) to identify the specific reading skills that learners need help with.

RMA (Rapid Mathematics Assessment)

An assessment tool designed to quickly evaluate the numeracy skills of learners and identify their proficiency levels in Math.

ARAL (Academic Recovery and Accessible Learning)

A DepEd program aimed at addressing learning gaps. In this system, students with low proficiency are automatically tagged as "ARAL Candidates" to receive focused intervention.

GST (Group Screening Test)

A component of the PHIL-IRI used to quickly screen a whole class to identify who among the students need further individualized testing.

Reading Profile

The classification of a student's reading level. It can be Independent (can read without help), Instructional (can read with assistance), or Frustration (struggles significantly).

E-Signature

A digital image of a teacher's or official's handwritten signature uploaded into the system to authorize and validate printed reports.

Consolidated Report

A single, unified report generated by the system that combines and summarizes the data submitted by all teachers across different grade levels and sections.

5. ARAL Identification & Intervention Process

To ensure that no learner is left behind, the AES Unified Reporting System has a built-in automatic tagging feature for the Academic Recovery and Accessible Learning (ARAL) Program. The system strictly follows the DepEd guidelines in identifying learners who need reading and numeracy interventions.

A. Who are automatically tagged as ARAL Candidates?

The system automatically flags a learner as an ARAL candidate based on their performance in the following assessments:

  • PHIL-IRI (GST): Learners who score 0 to 13 in the Group Screening Test. Regardless of their reading profile (Frustration, Instructional, or Independent), they are considered for ARAL because their reading level is below their current grade level.
  • CRLA (KS1): Learners who fall under the "Low Emerging" category.
  • RMA (Math): Learners who score below 50% (categorized as Low Proficient or Non Proficient).

B. The Intervention Process (Pre-test, MOSY, Post-test)

Once identified via GST or CRLA, these learners will undergo a deeper assessment to track their progress:

  1. Pre-Test: To establish the learner's baseline reading level at the start of the intervention.
  2. MOSY (Middle of School Year): To monitor their progress and adjust the intervention strategies if needed.
  3. Post-Test: To evaluate the effectiveness of the intervention at the end of the school year.

The system will automatically compute and show the progress of the learner from Pre-test to Post-test in the Consolidated Reports.

C. The ARAL Masterlist

All identified learners are automatically compiled in the ARAL Masterlist page. Teachers and coordinators can filter this list by Grade Level and Section, and print it for their physical records and parent conferences.

6. Guide for Teachers

A. Login and Uploading E-Signature

  1. Open the App and log in using the username and password provided by the ICT.
  2. At the top, click "✍️ E-Signatures".
  3. Upload a clear image of your signature (PNG format). This will automatically appear on all your reports.

B. Submitting Narrative and Activity Reports

  1. Go to Narrative Reports on the left sidebar.
  2. Select the Report Type (e.g., Narrative, Accomplishment, Project Proposal).
  3. Choose the Subject/Program (e.g., English, Math, Other).
  4. Enter your Position/Title and the Title of your report.
  5. Paste your full report content in the text box.
  6. Optional: Under "Pictorials", you can upload multiple images to serve as documentation of your activity.
  7. Click the "🚀 Submit Report" button. (Note: This goes directly to the School Head for final approval).

C. Submitting Assessments (PHIL-IRI, CRLA, RMA)

  1. Go to Data Entry and select the assessment type (e.g., PHIL-IRI).
  2. Enter the Grade and Section.
  3. For GST, simply enter Name, Total Score. For others (Pre-test, MOSY, Post-test), enter Name, Level Passed, Oral Level, Comp Level.
  4. Click "🔍 Parse Data". The Reading Profile and ARAL Tag will automatically appear.
  5. Click "🚀 Submit All Data" at the top right.

D. Viewing the ARAL Masterlist

The ARAL Masterlist automatically compiles all students from approved reports who are tagged as "ARAL Reading" or "ARAL Math" candidates.

  1. Go to ARAL Masterlist on the left sidebar.
  2. Use the dropdown menu to Filter by Grade Level or type a specific Section to view only your students.
  3. Click "🖨️ Print Masterlist" to generate a clean, formatted document for your intervention records.

E. Tracking Submissions and Approvals

  • My Submissions: Shows all the reports you have submitted, regardless of status. You can view the progress here.
  • My Approved Reports: This is your archive. All reports that have been fully approved by the School Head are stored here. You can view and print them anytime.

F. Editing a "Returned" Report

If a Coordinator or the School Head finds an error in your report, they will "Return" it to you with a comment.

  1. Go to My Submissions.
  2. Look for the report with a red badge that says "Returned to Teacher".
  3. Click the "✏️ Edit" button beside it.
  4. The system will automatically load your old data. Make the necessary corrections.
  5. Click Submit again to resubmit it for approval.

7. Guide for Coordinators

As a coordinator, you are the first to validate assessment reports before they reach the School Head.

  • Go to Teacher Submissions.
  • Click "👁️ View" to see the details.
  • If there are errors, type a comment and click "↩️ Return to Teacher".
  • If everything is in order, click "✅ Approve & Forward".
  • You can also view the ARAL Masterlist and filter by Grade/Section before printing.

8. Guide for ICT / Admin

Managing Users

Go to Manage Users. This is where you create teacher accounts and assign roles (Teacher, Coordinator, School Head). Always copy the auto-generated password and provide it to the user.

How to Clean the System (Before the New Year)

Go to System Settings and look for the red Danger Zone. Click "CLEAR ALL SUBMISSIONS". This will delete all reports (to start fresh) but will not affect your user accounts.

9. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

1. I forgot my password. How can I log in?

Please contact your ICT Coordinator or the School Head. They can go to the "Manage Users" section and click the "Reset Password" button to generate a new one for you.

2. I cannot see the "Install App" button in my browser.

Make sure you are using Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge. Look at the far right side of the address bar (where the website link is) for a small monitor icon with a downward arrow. If it is not there, refresh the page or check if the browser is updated.

3. My submission says "Pending" for a long time.

Reports under "Pending Coordinator Review" are waiting for your program coordinator to check them. Once they approve it, it will be forwarded to the School Head. You can monitor the status in "My Submissions".

4. Why did my report get returned?

If your report status is red and says "Returned to Teacher", it means there is an error in your data or your narrative content. Click the "Edit" button, read the coordinator's comment, fix the error, and resubmit.

5. The printed report has no DepEd or School Logo. What should I do?

Logos are saved directly in your browser. When you open the report preview, click the "📷 Upload DepEd Logo" and "📷 Upload School Logo" buttons at the top right corner before printing.

6. The print preview shows a blank "about:blank" header.

This is a browser default. In the print preview window, click "More settings" or "Options" and uncheck "Headers and footers".

7. Can I use the system on my phone?

Yes! The system is mobile-responsive. However, for heavy data entry and printing, it is highly recommended to use a laptop or desktop computer.

8. The system is running slow or not loading.

Please check your internet connection. The system relies on Google Sheets, so a stable connection is required to submit data. If the internet is fine, try pressing Ctrl + F5 to refresh the cache.
