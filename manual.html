Foreword

In the rapidly evolving landscape of basic education, the integration of technology into administrative and instructional processes has become not just an advantage, but a necessity. The Department of Education continues to champion innovations that streamline the workload of our educators, allowing them to focus on what truly matters: the learners.

The AES Unified Reporting System is a testament to this vision. Born out of the need to simplify the consolidation of reading and numeracy assessments (such as PHIL-IRI, CRLA, and RMA), this system automates data processing, ensures accuracy, and provides real-time insights for data-driven decision-making.

This manual serves as a comprehensive guide for our teachers, coordinators, and school heads in navigating the system. May this innovation empower our educators to work smarter and serve our learners better.

1. Getting Started

Welcome to the AES Unified Reporting System! This platform is designed to streamline the reporting of PHIL-IRI, CRLA, and RMA assessments, as well as Narrative Reports.

System Requirements

  • Stable internet connection.
  • Updated web browser (Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, or Mozilla Firefox).
  • Google Account (for narrative report photo uploads).

2. Logging In and Role Switching

To access the system:

  1. Open the provided URL link.
  2. Enter your assigned Username and Password.
  3. Click the Login button.

Note: If you hold multiple roles (e.g., Teacher and ICT Coordinator), you can switch between them using the dropdown menu located in the sidebar beneath your name.

Understanding the Interface

Once logged in, you will see the following main interface components:

  • Sidebar (Left): Contains your profile, role switcher, and the navigation menu for all modules (Data Entry, My Reports, System Settings).
  • Top Bar: Contains the title of your current page and quick action buttons such as Submit All Data or E-Signatures.
  • Workspace (Center): The main area where you input data, view tables, and generate reports.

3. Data Entry: PHIL-IRI (GST)

The system features an automated parsing tool for the PHIL-IRI Group Screening Test (GST).

  1. Go to the PHIL-IRI (KS2) module.
  2. Select the Language and Assessment Type.
  3. Copy your data from Excel/Sheets (Format: Name, Score).
  4. Paste it into the text box and click 🔍 Parse Data.
  5. The system will automatically calculate the Profile and suggest interventions.

Color Coding Guide:

  • Light Blue: Grade Ready (For Enrichment).
  • Green: Independent (For Remediation).
  • Yellow: Instructional (For Remediation).
  • Red: Frustration (For Remediation).

4. Data Entry: CRLA and RMA

Similar to PHIL-IRI, you can paste data for the CRLA (Comprehensive Rapid Literacy Assessment) and RMA (Rapid Mathematics Assessment). Ensure you paste the data in the correct format indicated in the respective modules.

5. Submitting Narrative Reports & Photos

For activity reports, accomplishments, or project proposals, navigate to the Narrative Reports tab. Fill in the title, content, and your position. You may also upload activity photos, which are securely stored in the school's Google Drive.

6. Coordinator Review and Approval

Program Coordinators can view pending submissions in the Teacher Submissions tab. They can either return the report to the teacher with a comment or approve and forward it to the School Head.

7. School Head Final Approval & Printing

The School Head can view forwarded reports and give final approval. Once approved, the report is locked and ready for printing with official DepEd letterheads and e-signatures.

8. System Settings (ICT)

The ICT Coordinator has access to System Settings. Here, you can:

  • Upload Logos: Set the DepEd and School logos for the official reports.
  • Change Script URL: Update the backend Google Apps Script link if necessary.
  • Clear All Submissions: Wipe the database and Google Drive photos at the end of the school year to prepare the system for a fresh start.

9. Managing the ARAL Masterlist

The ARAL Masterlist is an automated feature of the system that collects all learners requiring remediation in Reading and Math based on their assessment results.

How to view the ARAL Masterlist:

  1. Go to the 🌟 ARAL Masterlist menu.
  2. You can filter the list by Grade Level or Section.
  3. You will see two tables: ARAL Reading Candidates (from PHIL-IRI and CRLA) and ARAL Math Candidates (from RMA).
  4. The list includes their Profile/Level and Suggested Intervention to guide the teacher.
Note: Learners with a "Grade Ready" status are not included here as they are for Enrichment, not Remediation.

10. Printing Official Reports

The system has a built-in Print-Ready Format with DepEd letterheads and e-signatures. It is set to A4 Landscape paper size.

How to print a Report:

  1. Go to My Approved Reports (for Teachers) or Final School Report (for School Head).
  2. Click the 👁️ View button to see the report.
  3. Click the 🖨️ Print button.
  4. A new tab will open containing the formal document. Click Print (or press Ctrl + P).

Important Printing Reminders:

  • Ensure the printer is set to Landscape orientation.
  • Enable "Background Graphics" in your browser's print settings so the colors (Red, Yellow, Green, Blue) will appear in the table.
  • Logos and e-signatures automatically appear if they have been uploaded in the System Settings.

11. Troubleshooting & FAQs

Here are some common issues and how to resolve them:

1. "Invalid credentials" when logging in.

  • Solution: Check your username and password in Google Sheets. Ensure there are no extra spaces at the end of the password. The Status column must also be exactly active.

2. Logos are not appearing on the Report.

  • Solution: Go to System Settings (if you are ICT) and re-upload the DepEd and School Logos. Click "Save Logos".

3. The ARAL Tag is not showing even with a low score.

  • Solution: Make sure the correct Grade Level is entered in the Teacher Details. If "Kinder" is entered, it will not compute the ARAL Tag for GST because the formula is only for Grades 1 to 6. Use CRLA for Kinder.

4. The system is slow or not saving.

  • Solution: Check your internet connection. If uploading multiple photos, wait a few seconds as the images are being sent directly to Google Drive.

12. Data Privacy and Security

In accordance with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173), this system implements strict security measures for learner data.

  • Secure Storage: All assessment data is saved in Google Sheets owned by the school (not publicly accessible).
  • Photo Storage: Activity photos are saved in a private folder on Google Drive (AES_Narrative_Photos).
  • Access Control: Only authorized personnel (Teachers, Coordinators, School Head) with valid accounts can access the system.
Important Reminder: Do not share your username and password with anyone. Always log out after using the system, especially when using a public or shared computer.

13. Glossary of Terms

Familiarize yourself with these common acronyms used in the system:

  • PHIL-IRI: Philippine Informal Reading Inventory (Used for Grades 3-6).
  • GST: Group Screening Test (Initial phase of PHIL-IRI).
  • CRLA: Comprehensive Rapid Literacy Assessment (Used for Grades 1-3 / Kinder).
  • RMA: Rapid Mathematics Assessment.
  • ARAL: Academic Recovery and Accessible Learning (Intervention program).
  • Enrichment: Activities for students who are "Grade Ready" or Independent readers.
  • Remediation: Targeted interventions for students struggling with reading or math.
  • E-Signature: Digital signature used for official report approvals.
